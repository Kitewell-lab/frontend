# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in Helios Lab
a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness
- Being respectful of differing opinions
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others’ private information
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report issues to the maintainers via GitHub. Maintainers will review and act on
reports they consider appropriate, including warning or banning contributors.
